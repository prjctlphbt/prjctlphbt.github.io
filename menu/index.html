Our Menu - Authentic Beijing Cuisine

Discover the flavors of authentic Beijing cuisine at Beijing Restaurant in Washington, DC. Since 1985, we've brought traditional recipes and time-honored techniques to your table.

Signature Dishes That Tell a Story

Every dish on our menu represents authentic Beijing traditions. Our signature offerings showcase the rich flavors and techniques passed down through generations of Chinese culinary masters.

From savory dumplings crafted with paper-thin wrappers to perfectly seasoned stir-fries that balance sweet, salty, and umami notes, each plate delivers an unforgettable dining experience. Our chefs use traditional wok techniques and authentic seasonings to create dishes that honor Beijing's centuries-old culinary heritage.

Chef Specialties and Traditional Favorites

Our experienced culinary team brings decades of expertise to every meal. We prepare each dish with premium ingredients sourced for quality and authentic seasonings imported directly from China.

Popular menu highlights include:

  • Traditional Beijing-style hand-pulled noodles with rich, aromatic broths
  • Hand-rolled dumplings filled with seasoned pork, vegetables, or seafood
  • Aromatic roasted duck prepared using time-honored Beijing methods
  • Seasonal vegetable preparations featuring fresh, locally-sourced produce
  • Classic soup selections including hot and sour soup and wonton soup
  • Szechuan-style dishes for those who enjoy bold, spicy flavors

Family-Style Dining Experience

We believe great food brings families together. Our menu features generous portions perfect for sharing with loved ones, following the traditional Chinese approach to communal dining.

Many dishes are designed for family-style service, encouraging conversation and connection around the table. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion like a birthday or anniversary, hosting a business dinner, or enjoying a casual weeknight meal, our offerings create memorable moments that bring people closer together.

Dietary Accommodations Available

We welcome guests with different dietary needs and preferences. Our kitchen can prepare many dishes to accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-sensitive diets without compromising on flavor or authenticity.

Our experienced team works closely with you to ensure everyone at your table enjoys their dining experience. We offer extensive vegetarian options featuring tofu, fresh vegetables, and plant-based proteins, and can modify preparations when possible to meet specific dietary requirements.

Fresh Ingredients and Quality Standards

Quality ingredients form the foundation of every dish we serve. We source fresh vegetables daily, select premium cuts of meat and seafood, and use authentic Chinese cooking techniques to preserve natural flavors and nutritional value.

Our commitment to quality extends to our preparation methods. We hand-roll our noodles fresh daily, prepare sauces from scratch using traditional recipes, and maintain strict quality standards in our kitchen to ensure consistency in every meal.

Reservation and Ordering Guidance

Ready to experience authentic Beijing cuisine? We make it easy to enjoy our menu offerings whether you're dining in or taking out.

For the best dining experience, we recommend making reservations during peak hours, especially on weekends and holidays. Our knowledgeable team is happy to help you select dishes that match your preferences and dietary needs, and can recommend wine pairings to complement your meal.
