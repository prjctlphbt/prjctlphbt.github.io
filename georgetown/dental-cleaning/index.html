Frequently Asked Questions About dental cleaning in Georgetown

What is included in a professional dental cleaning?

A professional dental cleaning at Dental Studio includes plaque and tartar removal, teeth polishing, and fluoride treatment. Our dental hygienists thoroughly clean areas that are difficult to reach with regular brushing and flossing.

How often should I get my teeth cleaned?

Most patients benefit from dental cleanings every six months. However, some individuals may need more frequent cleanings based on their oral health needs and risk factors.

Does dental cleaning hurt?

Professional dental cleanings are typically comfortable procedures. Some patients may experience mild sensitivity, especially if it's been a while since their last cleaning.

How long does a dental cleaning take?

A routine dental cleaning usually takes 30-60 minutes, depending on the amount of buildup and your individual oral health needs. Contact Dental Studio in Washington to schedule your appointment.
