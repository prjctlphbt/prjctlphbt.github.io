Frequently Asked Questions About dental implants in East Austin

What are dental implants? Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They provide a strong foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures, offering a permanent solution for tooth loss.

How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene are essential for long-term success.

Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most healthy adults with adequate bone density are candidates. Our team will evaluate your oral health and bone structure to determine if implants are right for you.
