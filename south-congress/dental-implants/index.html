Frequently Asked Questions About dental implants in South Congress

What are dental implants? Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They provide a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures, offering a permanent solution for tooth replacement.

How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene are essential for long-term success.

Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. Our team at Dental Studio evaluates each patient individually to determine the best treatment approach.

How do I schedule a consultation? Contact us today to schedule your dental implant consultation at our Austin location.
