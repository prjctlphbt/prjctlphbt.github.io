Contact Us

We'd love to hear from you

We'd love to hear from you! Whether you have questions about our authentic Beijing cuisine, want to make a reservation for our famous Peking duck, or need information about our hand-pulled noodles, our friendly team is here to help. Don't hesitate to reach out anytime.

What to Expect

When you contact Beijing Restaurant, you can expect a warm response from our knowledgeable staff who are passionate about sharing the flavors of Beijing with Washington DC. We typically respond to calls during business hours and aim to reply to emails within 24 hours. For table reservations, we recommend calling directly to ensure availability, especially for weekend dining or larger groups. Our team will be happy to discuss menu options, dietary accommodations, or help you plan your perfect dining experience with us.
