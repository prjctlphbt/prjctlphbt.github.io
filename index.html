Our Story

Beijing Restaurant has been serving Washington DC's finest Chinese cuisine since 1985. Our journey began when Master Chef Li brought his family's traditional recipes from Beijing to share with the local community.

Today, we continue to honor those traditions while creating new memories for families and friends. Every meal is prepared with care, using time-honored techniques and the finest ingredients.

From our famous Peking duck to our hand-pulled noodles, each dish represents our commitment to authentic flavors and exceptional quality.
